MUT/HUR Publisher helps creators review vertical photo content, connect their TikTok account, and send an approved draft to TikTok Inbox.

01 / CONNECT

Your account, your consent.

Connect using TikTok Login Kit. See the connected account before sending content.

02 / REVIEW

Approve every image.

Preview 9:16 photos and their order, from cover to carousel. Sending starts only when you select Send to TikTok.

03 / FINISH

Complete the post in TikTok.

Open the Inbox notification in TikTok to edit, confirm, and decide whether to post.

The TikTok integration is in setup and Sandbox testing. Draft transfer uses the Content Posting API. Availability depends on TikTok permissions and app review.

A deliberate publishing workflow

The desktop app shows transfer progress and keeps a publish identifier for status checks. Sending a draft does not publish it to your account. The creator retains the final posting decision in TikTok.

This website provides information and policies for the desktop app; it does not collect your TikTok login or offer a hosted publishing dashboard.
