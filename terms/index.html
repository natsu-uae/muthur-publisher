Terms of Service

MUT/HUR Publisher is a desktop content review and draft transfer tool managed by Natsuki. By using the tool, you agree to these terms.

What the tool does

You can connect a TikTok account, review 4:5 feed photos, and explicitly send a selected photo draft to TikTok Inbox using TikTok's Content Posting API. You finish editing and decide whether to post within TikTok. The current integration is in setup and Sandbox testing and depends on TikTok's permissions and review.

Your content and account

Use only accounts you are authorized to connect and content you have the rights and necessary consent to share. You retain ownership of your content. You permit the tool to process selected images and make them temporarily accessible on the publishing media host so TikTok can retrieve them.

Your final decision

Review the destination account, image order, and content before sending. Sending a draft does not automatically publish a post. Follow TikTok's terms, community guidelines, and requirements, including appropriate disclosure for AI-generated content.

Availability and responsibility

The tool is provided as available. Transfers may fail, be delayed, or be restricted by TikTok. No uninterrupted service or successful publication is promised. Keep your original files and protect access to your computer and accounts.

Stopping use

You may stop using the tool at any time and revoke its access in TikTok's app permissions or use Disconnect TikTok in the desktop app. Read the Privacy Policy for data handling and local deletion instructions.

Changes

Updated terms will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Review them before continued use.
