Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the desktop MUT/HUR Publisher tool, managed by Natsuki, handles information for its TikTok draft transfer workflow.

Information processed

When you connect TikTok, the tool receives the basic account identifier, display name, and profile image URL allowed by user.info.basic, and access and refresh tokens with their expiry information. It processes your selected images, their order, any title or description you provide, and transfer identifiers and status.

Purpose and storage

Account information identifies the destination account. Tokens authorize draft uploads and status checks and allow renewal until access is revoked or expires. Tokens are encrypted locally using Windows user protection (DPAPI). Client credentials are read from local secret configuration. Sandbox and Production data are kept separately.

The tool keeps local content and transfer records to show progress, resume status checks, and prevent duplicate sends. It does not request your TikTok password, advertising data, contacts, or private messages. Secrets are excluded from application logs and source control.

Images and third parties

Selected draft images are placed on a publicly accessible HTTPS media host so TikTok can fetch them. Anyone with the image URL may retrieve the image while it is available. Do not select sensitive content for this workflow.

TikTok receives the selected images and draft metadata after you explicitly choose Send to TikTok. The website and publishing media are hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting providers and TikTok may process technical request data under their own policies. This site does not add analytics, advertising trackers, or application cookies.

Retention and removal

Local records remain until you remove them. Public media are retained during transfer and may be removed through the tool's cleanup command after TikTok confirms receipt and at least 24 hours have passed. Hosting deployment history may retain earlier copies; removal from the current website does not erase provider history or copies already received by TikTok.

To stop access, use Disconnect TikTok in the desktop app or revoke access in TikTok's app permissions. After revoking access, you can remove the local STORAGE/TIKTOK data directory to delete tokens and job records. Keep job records while any transfer is unresolved to preserve duplicate protection. Deleting local data does not delete content already delivered to TikTok.

Your choices

Connecting TikTok and sending content are separate actions. You can decline authorization, choose different content, or decide not to send or post. Final editing and posting take place in TikTok.

Changes

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date.
